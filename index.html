spatiAlert Toolkit

Free tools for finding hotspots of under-vaccination in school-level data. Two browser tools handle the hardest part, cleaning and preparing the data, and a Shiny app finds the hotspots.

Work through them in order, or use whichever one you need.

1PrepareHTML · runs in browser

RecordMatcher

Matches records between two files that don't necessarily share a clean ID, for example matching school vaccination data to one or more school address or school enrollment files using school name. It proposes a match for every record, lets you review as many or as few as you choose, and merges the data.

  • Fuzzy matching on one or more columns
  • Every export row keeps its score and how it was decided
  • Built-in clean-up step before export
Download Open online
2PrepareHTML · runs in browser

PlaceFinder

Turns addresses into latitude and longitude. Build the address from your columns, look it up, fix the ones that fail, and export coordinates with the source of each one recorded.

  • Uses the US Census geocoder
  • Flags addresses that need fixing
  • Each row records where its coordinates came from
Download Open online
3AnalyzeR Shiny · download to run

spatiAlert

Calculates Getis-Ord Gi* hotspots using point or area-aggregated data.

  • Census tract and county data for all 50 states are pre-loaded, or upload your own shapefiles (such as school districts)
  • Explore first: map schools, counts of undervaccinated students, and vaccination rates
  • Export the exploration maps and a final report with tables and explanations
  • Runs locally in R on your computer
